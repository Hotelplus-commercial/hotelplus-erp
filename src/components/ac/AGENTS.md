## Production Report drop + monthly cut-off — PR-O1 (closed 6 Nov 2026) · PR1–PR18
- One month rule (all sources): the invoice of billing month M+1 charges the commission of service month M (commissionServiceMonth); commission lines carry `service_month`. Report source for M: ready drop item (customer-confirmed) wins over AC's manual CRM report of the same contract + SKU + month (mergeDropReports, prDropReports); never a current-month report. Flat-only contracts keep their old report timing. Guard: a service month any non-void invoice already charged is never charged again (old invoices = their billing month, chargedServiceMonths). A renewal's first invoice charges no commission (that month is the old contract's); an ended contract gets one last commission-only item for its final month. Issued invoices never change. Unbilled past months are never auto-billed (AC_OPEN_NOTES in src/lib/ac-open-notes.ts shows the note).
- Drop items = pure src/lib/pr-drop.ts (contract × service month, key contract|month, created from the 1st of M+1, cut-off the 3rd of M+1 = PR_DROP_DUE_DAY; statuses derived, never stored); store src/lib/pr-drop-store.ts = collection meridia.production_report_drops.v1 → Cloud ps:production_report_drops; PDF bytes in the Partner on-device file store (ref PRD-…). Sent to AC + no reply by the cut-off = ลูกค้ายืนยัน (อัตโนมัติ); dispute = not confirmed; locked after พร้อมวางบิล until AC ส่งกลับแก้ (reason). Revenue only for ORM commission contracts; a differing AC manual revenue is kept (ac_prev_revenue) with an amber warning until AC รับทราบ.
- Views on /ac/production-report: ORM / MKT sender (?mode=drop) · ORM_LEAD / GRM / MKT_LEAD overview (counts only, leadRow has no money, เตือนผู้ดูแล = one bell per item per day) · AC / HOC / MD full page + PrAcPanel. Leads do not send on behalf (revenue would expose money). Bells: prBellsDue keys include the day; sender = DEPT_QUEUE ORM / MARCOM, lead = ROLE GRM / MARCOM_LEAD. DEMO = src/lib/pr-drop-demo.ts (ก.ย. 69 only, memory).
